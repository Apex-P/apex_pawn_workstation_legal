2. Introductory Statement and Purpose of Agreement

Apex Pawn is designed as a practical business operations platform for pawn shops and related regulated businesses. It is intended to help pawnbrokers computerize and manage core pawn-shop activities, including collateral intake, buy transactions, pawn and layaway payments, customer management, inventory tracking, collateral forfeiture, employee management, store analytics, law-enforcement data reporting, firearm acquisition and disposition records, item labeling, receipt printing, pawn-ticket printing, in-store money management, and related operational functions.

It is not a substitute for Platform Licensee’s own legal counsel, compliance judgment, regulatory obligations, law-enforcement reporting duties, firearm compliance obligations, privacy obligations, data-security obligations, or business responsibility.
