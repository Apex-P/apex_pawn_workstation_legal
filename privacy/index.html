Information We Collect

Account and Employee Information

To create and manage Accounts, We collect information about a Store's employees and other users, such as names, email addresses, login credentials, employee PINs, roles and permissions, and time-clock records.

Billing Information

We bill Stores by invoice. To do so, We collect each Store's billing contact information and keep records of invoices and payments.

Store Data

Depending on how a Store uses the Service, Store Data may include:

  • Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and dates of birth
  • Customer physical descriptions, including sex, race, ethnicity, height, weight, hair color, and eye color
  • Government identification information, such as driver's license details
  • Photographs of customers and items
  • Each customer's pawn, buy, sale, layaway, and payment history, including descriptions and serial numbers of the items involved
  • Each customer's store credit balance and history
  • Messages exchanged between the Store and its customers who use Pawnshop Pal
  • Firearm acquisition and disposition records

QuickBooks Data

If a Store connects the Service to QuickBooks Online, We collect and store:

  • The QuickBooks company identifier and the authorization tokens that QuickBooks issues when the Store grants access
  • The Store's chart of accounts: each account's identifier, name, type, subtype, classification, and whether it is active
  • The Store's choice of which QuickBooks account each Apex Pawn accounting category posts to
  • A record of each synchronization, including its date, status, any error message, and the identifier of the journal entry it created in QuickBooks

Diagnostic and Usage Data

When the Application encounters an error, it sends an error report to Our error-monitoring provider. Error reports include technical details such as the Application version, operating system, device information, and the sequence of events leading up to the error. For a sample of activity, the Application also sends performance data to the same provider, such as which screens were opened and how long screens and operations took to load, whether or not an error occurred.

Our hosting provider keeps server logs of requests made to the Service, including IP addresses, request times, and other request details.

The Application also checks for and downloads software updates, which involves sending the Application version, platform, and a random identifier for the installation to Our update provider.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including storing Store Data and producing the records, reports, receipts, and labels a Store requests
  • Manage Accounts, subscriptions, and billing
  • Synchronize accounting information with QuickBooks Online when a Store has connected it
  • Diagnose and fix errors, and improve the performance and reliability of the Service
  • Provide customer support, onboarding, and data migration
  • Protect the security of the Service, and prevent fraud, abuse, and unlawful use
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce the Agreement

We may use aggregated or de-identified information that does not identify any Store or individual for analytics, product improvement, research, and development, including the development of AI-based features. We do not use identifiable Store Data to train AI models without the Store's consent.

How We Use QuickBooks Data

We access a Store's QuickBooks Online company only after an authorized user of that Store connects it, and only to provide the QuickBooks integration to that Store. Specifically:

  • We read the Store's chart of accounts so that the Store can choose which QuickBooks accounts its Apex Pawn activity posts to.
  • We periodically create journal entries in the Store's QuickBooks company that summarize each day of the Store's activity, starting from the date the Store chooses to synchronize from. If the Store moves that date earlier, or reconnects after a period of being disconnected, We also create journal entries for the days that have not yet been synchronized. Each journal entry contains the date, a debit or credit amount for each mapped account, the name of the Apex Pawn accounting category for each line, and an internal note identifying the Apex Pawn synchronization that created it.

Journal entries contain totals only. We do not send the names, contact details, identification information, or other Personal Data of a Store's customers to QuickBooks.

We do not sell QuickBooks Data, share it with third parties for their own purposes, or use it for advertising.

A Store can disconnect QuickBooks at any time from within the Application or from within QuickBooks Online. When a Store disconnects from within the Application, We ask QuickBooks to revoke Our access and delete the Store's QuickBooks authorization tokens, its stored chart of accounts, and its account mappings. When a Store disconnects from within QuickBooks Online, We delete the same data once We confirm with QuickBooks that Our access has been revoked; if We cannot confirm it, the Store can disconnect from within the Application to remove it. We keep the record of past synchronizations and the QuickBooks company identifier they refer to, so that the Store can see what was sent to QuickBooks and so that reconnecting the same QuickBooks company resumes where it left off.

How We Share Information

We do not sell Personal Data. We share information as follows:

  • With Service Providers that help Us operate the Service, under obligations to protect it, including:
    • Supabase, which hosts Our database, authentication, and server functions, and stores Store Data and QuickBooks Data
    • Sentry, which receives error reports
    • Shorebird, which delivers Application updates
  • With services a Store chooses to use, at that Store's direction:
    • Intuit, when a Store connects QuickBooks Online, as described above
    • eBay, when a user searches eBay listings to research an item's market value. We send only the search terms and item condition the user enters.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which receives the text of each message a Store sends to a customer who uses Pawnshop Pal, in order to deliver it to that customer as a notification
    • Law-enforcement reporting services, such as LeadsOnline. The Application generates reports that the Store then submits to these services itself; We do not send data to them.
  • With professional advisors, such as lawyers, accountants, and insurers, under duties of confidentiality, where needed for their advice or services to Us.
  • With the Store's consent or at its direction.
  • For legal reasons. We disclose information when required to by law, subpoena, court order, or other legal process. We refer law-enforcement requests for Store Data to the Store where We are permitted to, and We notify the affected Store of any such disclosure unless the law prohibits it. We may also disclose information where necessary to prevent fraud or a security incident affecting the Service, or in an emergency involving a risk of death or serious physical injury.
  • For business transfers. If We are involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of all or part of Our business, information may be transferred to the successor as part of that transaction, together with Our obligations under the Agreement and this Privacy Policy. We will notify affected Stores before their information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Retention of Information

We keep Store Data for as long as the Store's subscription is active. After a subscription ends, We use commercially reasonable efforts to keep Store Data for up to ninety days so the Store can export it, after which it may be deleted. We may keep some records longer where reasonably necessary for backups, billing and accounting, dispute resolution, legal holds, security investigations, fraud prevention, or compliance with law.

QuickBooks authorization tokens, chart-of-accounts data, and account mappings are deleted when a Store disconnects QuickBooks, as described above.

Each Store is responsible for keeping the pawn, firearm, tax, and other business records that the law requires it to keep.

Security of Information

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information We hold. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Access to QuickBooks authorization tokens is restricted to Our server functions; they cannot be read by users of the Application. We notify affected Stores of a confirmed security breach involving their data within seventy-two hours of discovering it, unless the law requires otherwise.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Stores are responsible for securing their own devices, networks, and user accounts, including promptly removing access for former employees.
